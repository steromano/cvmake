## Degrees

### Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Mathematical Physics
**Institution:** International School of Advanced Studies (SISSA)
**Location:** Trieste, Italy
**Period:** 2008 - 2012
**Supervisor:** Prof. Boris Dubrovin

**About SISSA:**
Probably the second most prestigious postdoctoral school for math/physics in Italy (after Scuola Normale Superiore). Well known internationally with many distinguished faculty. Chose SISSA in part because university mates went there and spoke highly of it.

**Dissertation:** "Special Frobenius Structures on Hurwitz Spaces and Applications"
Focused on mathematics at the intersection of string theory, algebraic geometry, and integrable systems. Highly theoretical work.

**Published Research:**
- Multiple papers in mathematical physics journals (see achievements.md for full list)
- Presented at international conferences

**Career Note:**
By end of PhD, was already skeptical about pursuing academic career (lacked raw motivation, lifestyle implications of postdoc circuit). Did one postdoc to "try it out" but confirmed within months it wasn't the right path. Started preparing for industry transition.
